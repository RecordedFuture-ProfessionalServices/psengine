### Note

As of June 11, 2024, Insikt Group identified several indicators of compromise (IOCs) associated with Scattered Spider phishing infrastructure. Scattered Spider is an English-speaking financially motivated cybercriminal group with members likely located in North America and the UK. We assess that this threat activity is a continuation of a campaign previously identified in April 2024. For more information about this campaign, please see the “Scattered Spider Infrastructure Note: April 2024 Phishing Domains and Servers” Insikt Research Lead attached in the Validation URL of this note.
